# Why the Rich Borrow Money They Don't Need

**Logline:** By the end, the viewer understands the three legal steps (buy, borrow, die) that let large fortunes grow with little income tax, why they work, what they cost, and the one piece of the same rulebook that is open to ordinary people: the long term capital gains rate, including its 0% bracket for 2026.

**The loop:** How can a fortune grow by twenty four billion dollars while its owner pays about a tenth of one percent of that in federal income tax, legally? And which part of that rulebook is open to you?

**The turn:** The rich own things that grow, and avoid selling them.
