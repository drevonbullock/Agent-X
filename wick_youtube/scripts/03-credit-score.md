# The Invisible Number That Runs Your Life

**Logline:** You'll understand what a FICO credit score is, where it came from, the five ingredients inside it and their weights, how utilization works, which myths are false, and what the number really costs, so you can see it for what it is: a record of trust, not wealth.

**The loop:** If a credit score has no idea how much money you make, what is it actually measuring?

**The turn:** A credit score doesn't measure wealth. It measures trust.

## CH3 · The Full Card Problem

**B21** · `STAGE` · music: warm
VO: How much you're using has a name. Utilization. It's your card balance divided by your card limit. Say Wick has a card with a thousand dollar limit and he's carrying nine hundred. His utilization is ninety percent.
VISUAL: A flat credit card drawn as a glass tube with a scale from 0 to $1,000. Ink fills it to $900. A big "90%" label pops beside it. Wick (weary) holds the card up to the light like a thermometer.
ON-SCREEN: $900 ÷ $1,000 = 90% used

**B22** · `COUNTER` · music: warm
VO: To the formula, a card near its limit looks like strain, even if Wick has never missed a payment. For comparison, myFICO says people with scores above eight hundred use about seven percent of their available credit.
VISUAL: Counter drops from "90%" down to "7%" as the ink in the tube drains. A tiny row of high scoring candles in the background, each with a card only a sliver full, waves politely.
ON-SCREEN: 800+ scorers use about 7%

**B23** · `CHART_BAR` · music: warm

VISUAL: Two cards side by side, each a glass tube with a $1,000 limit, one holding $300 of ink, one empty. A combined bar above reads "$300 of $2,000 = 15%". Wick (alarmed) snips the empty card with scissors; the combined bar redraws as "$300 of $1,000 = 30%" and jumps up.

**B24** · `SPLIT` · music: warm
VO: Which kills the first myth. In one survey, almost half of people believed carrying a balance helps. It doesn't. You don't need to carry a balance to build your score. You can use the card and pay it off in full every month. Carrying a balance just means paying interest for nothing.
VISUAL: Split screen. Left: "MYTH: carry a balance" with Wick (alarmed) handing coins labeled "interest" to a smug card. Right: "TRUE: pay in full" with Wick (happy) swiping the card, then wiping the ink back to zero with a sponge. A big red X stamps the left side.
