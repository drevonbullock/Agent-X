# The Poor Buy Things. The Rich Buy Machines.

**Logline:** Everything you own either puts money in your pocket or takes it out, and the people who build wealth buy the machines that pay them first, then let those machines pay for everything else.

**The loop:** A starving farmer in February sits next to a full sack of perfectly good corn and refuses to eat it. Why would a full sack of food be the one thing he must not eat?

**The turn:** Never eat your seed corn.

## CH4 · Eating the Seed

**B26** · `STAGE` · music: tension
VO: Every machine has a February. A month when the car breaks and the rent goes up. When that fund balance starts to look like a vacation. The machine is right there, quiet and patient. And selling it would feel so good.

**B27** · `SPLIT` · music: tension
VO: Here's the math of eating the seed. Say Wick sells that ten thousand dollar fund to pay for a trip. He gets one great week. And he gives up the two hundred dollars a year it was paying him. Not once. Every year he would have owned it.
VISUAL: Split screen. Left: Wick, expression happy, on a flat beach with a drink, one sunny week. Right: a calendar fast forwards year after year, and each year a $200 envelope arrives at an empty mailbox and gets stamped "RETURN TO SENDER". Label: "Illustrative".
ON-SCREEN: 1 week vs $200, every year

**B28** · `STAGE` · music: warm

VISUAL: Back in the barn, now autumn. Wick the farmer, expression happy, eats from a big bowl of corn at the table. In the corner, the seed sack sits on a small pedestal with a velvet rope around it, like a museum exhibit. The mouse guards the rope.
ON-SCREEN: Eat the harvest. Keep the seed.

**B29** · `CHART_LINE` · music: warm

VISUAL: A line chart drawn as a row of fields across the screen, each field a little wider than the last, each with more stalks. A line connecting the tops of the stalks curves upward. Wick, expression proud, carries a small sack from one field's barn to the next field.
ON-SCREEN: Reinvest: plant part of the harvest
