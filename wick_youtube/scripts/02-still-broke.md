# Why You're Still Broke After Every Raise

**Logline:** Through a philosopher's red robe, a treadmill and three of Wick's raises, you'll understand lifestyle creep and the hedonic treadmill, why so many high earners still live paycheck to paycheck, and how to make a raise actually turn into wealth.

**The loop:** Where does every raise go?

**The turn:** Wealth is the money you didn't turn into a lifestyle.

## CH2 · The Treadmill

ON-SCREEN: Week 1. Month 3.

**B17** · `MACHINE` · music: warm

## CH3 · The Ratchet

**B22** · `MACHINE` · music: tension
VO: Because spending works like a ratchet. A ratchet is a gear that can click forward, but locks when you try to turn it back. Every raise clicks your spending forward. Clicking it back feels like losing something.
VISUAL: A big flat ratchet gear with a spring loaded catch. Each time a "raise" coin drops into a slot, the gear clicks forward one tooth: click, click, click. Wick (focused) tries to push the gear backward with both mittens. The catch holds. He slides down, feet scrabbling.
ON-SCREEN: Spending only clicks forward
SFX: tick

**B23** · `FLOW` · music: tension
VO: Here's the trick inside it. A raise shows up once. But the upgrades bill you every month. Rent. The car payment. Each subscription. The raise turns into a set of monthly promises to other people.
VISUAL: A single big "raise" coin enters the diagram from the left. It splits into many thin arrows heading to boxes labeled "Landlord", "Car loan", "Streaming", "Phone plan", "Delivery app". The arrows keep pulsing month after month (a small month counter ticks beside them) even after the coin is long gone.
ON-SCREEN: Paid once. Billed forever.
