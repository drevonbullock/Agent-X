# The Two Candles

**Logline:** A second person parable about two identical candles, one that burns to be seen and one that lights others, that reveals how compounding works and why every paycheck is a choice between them.

**The loop:** Two candles are lit from the same flame. One of them is you. Which one?

**The turn:** Every paycheck, you choose which candle you are.

## CH3 · The Quiet One

**B15** · `STAGE` · music: wonder
VO: The second candle did something odd with its very first spark. It kept most of it. But it took a small part, and it leaned over and touched it to the wick of a cold, unlit candle standing nearby.
VISUAL: Back to the first spark, in flashback tint. The right Wick, the Quiet One, lets most of its spark sink into its own flame, then tips a small piece of fire toward a short, dark candle stub beside it. The stub's wick catches with a soft pop. Expression focused.
SFX: pop

**B16** · `STAGE` · music: wonder
VO: Here is the strange thing about fire. When one candle lights another, it loses nothing. Its own flame is exactly as big as before. There are simply two flames now, where there was one.
VISUAL: Side by side: the Quiet One and the newly lit candle. A small amber measuring bracket appears beside the Quiet One's flame, before and after, showing the same height. A plus sign pops between the two lights. Expression curious, then happy.
ON-SCREEN: Lighting another costs you nothing.

**B17** · `STAGE` · music: warm
VO: It kept doing this, spark after spark. Most of each spark for itself. A small piece forward, to a new candle. It burned a little lower than the Bright One. From across the valley, it did not look like much.
VISUAL: Montage of sparks falling. Each time the Quiet One keeps most and tips a small piece to a new stub, lighting a slowly growing cluster of small candles behind it. Background gag: a lone moth flies up to it, holds up a scorecard reading 4, and flutters off toward the Bright One. The Quiet One shrugs. Expression calm.

**B18** · `STAGE` · music: tension
VO: It was not easy. With every spark, the Quiet One felt the pull to burn brighter. It watched the moths circle the other candle. Some nights it wondered if it was the fool. It sent a small flame forward anyway. Not all of it. Just a piece. Every time.
VISUAL: Night, a spark hovering in the Quiet One's hand. It looks across the ledge at the Bright One ringed by moths and admirers, then down at the dark stub beside it. A long pause. The spark wobbles. Then the Quiet One tips a small piece forward. Expression weary, then determined.
ON-SCREEN: Just a piece. Every time.

**B19** · `CHART_LINE` · music: tension

ON-SCREEN: Early on, bright looks better.

**B20** · `STAGE` · music: wonder

**B23** · `FLOW` · music: wonder

**B24** · `TEXT` · music: wonder
VO: Money works the same way. That is what compounding means. Money you invest earns a return, which is money it makes for you. Then that return earns a return of its own. Light that lights light. Nothing about it is clever. It asks for only two things. Something to start with, and time.
VISUAL: Handwritten amber text in the dark: "Compounding." Under it, a tiny flame lights a second flame, which lights a third, each labeled: "your money," "its return," "the return's return."

ON-SCREEN: 72 ÷ 7% = about 10 years
