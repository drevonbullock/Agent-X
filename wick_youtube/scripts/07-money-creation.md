# Where Money Actually Comes From

**Logline:** Most money is not printed by governments or lent out from savers. It is created by ordinary banks the moment someone signs a loan, and it disappears when the loan is repaid.

**The loop:** A man signs a car loan and thirty thousand dollars appears that did not exist a minute ago. Where did it come from, and where does it go when he pays it back?

**The turn:** Most money is someone's debt. Know which side you're on.

## CH2 · The Brakes

**B11** · `MACHINE` · music: tension

**B12** · `MACHINE` · music: tension

## CH3 · The Throttle

**B15** · `STAGE` · music: wonder

**B16** · `FLOW` · music: wonder
