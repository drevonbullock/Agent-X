But the sharper problem is sequencing. The course's strategy order (Section 10)

chain. If it isn't, you're measuring for the sake of measuring."

There is no business goal at the top of this chain. **That is the single
highest-priority gap in the whole system, and it is not one I can invent.**

## THE QUALITATIVE HOLE

The course splits data in two: **quantitative tells you what happened,
qualitative tells you why.** Sources for qualitative are surveys, interviews,
comments, and reviews.

This account has:
- **Quantitative:** yes, but thin — 46 posts, median 27 reach.
- **Qualitative:** effectively **zero**. One comment, ever. No surveys. No
  interviews. No DMs logged.

So the personas in `wick-audience.md` are built from published research, which
the course accepts as legitimate, but they are **unvalidated against real
humans.** They are a hypothesis, not a finding, and every agent should treat
them that way until qualitative data exists.

**The cheapest fix the course names: ask.** Surveys and interviews. With 41
followers, Dre can DM every single one and get real answers in an afternoon.
That is a higher-quality dataset than anything this account will generate
organically for months.

## GEOGRAPHIC LOCATION — the attribute I missed

The course lists it first among the things to learn, and the persona file
skipped it entirely. It matters more here than it looks, because **money
content is jurisdictional**: currency, typical rent, typical wage, credit
norms, and banking mechanics all differ by country, and a post built on US
numbers reads as wrong to a UK viewer.

**RESOLVED 2026-09-12: US.** Instagram's own demographics endpoint returns empty
(Meta suppresses it below ~100 followers), so this was a decision rather than a
lookup. Dre chose US: largest English-language money market, and it matches the
dollars already in the posts.

Consequence now that the lane is wealth building: country-specific mechanics are
IN SCOPE rather than banned. US credit scoring, US resale marketplaces, US card
terms. Revisit if the demographics endpoint later shows a different reality.

## POSITIONING STATEMENT — confirmed 2026-09-12

Rewritten after Dre pivoted the page to wealth building.

> For **people who want to build wealth but were never taught how money
> actually works**, who need **concrete methods and real arithmetic rather than
> motivation**, **Wick's Wisdom** provides **how to earn it, how credit moves
> it, and how compounding grows it — every claim proved with numbers you can
> check**, and **never names a ticker, platform or product to buy**, because
> **the mechanics that build wealth were never hidden, just never taught.**

That last clause is the brand promise. The guardrail clause is what keeps it
from becoming the thousandth finance-guru account: the page explains how the
machine works and refuses to tell anyone which button to press.

## THE FUNNEL — currently absent

The course's social media funnel (Section 5) is Awareness → Consideration →
Action → Engagement → Advocacy. This account produces **only awareness
content**. There is no middle.

Mapped for a content brand where the "conversion" is a follow, not a purchase:

| Stage | What it means here | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | A non-follower sees a Reel | 46 posts, all of them this |
| **Consideration** | They check the profile: bio, pinned posts, grid | ❌ nothing built for this |
| **Action** | They follow | 41 total |
| **Engagement** | They come back, save, reply | 24 shares / 1 comment total |
| **Advocacy** | They send a post to someone | 24 shares total |

**The gap that costs the most: consideration.** When the 1,444-reach post hit,
every person who tapped the profile landed on a grid of 40 near-identical
carousels whose captions all opened with the same three words. The course's
whole point about consideration is that it is where you *stand out from
competitors* — and there was nothing there to convert the one burst of
attention this account has ever received.

**Fix, in order:** a bio that states the promise (Section 26 — creative,
relevant, keyword-aware), three pinned posts that are the best teaching
examples, and a grid that does not read as one post repeated.

## CONTENT TYPE MIX — one of three

The course names three types (Section 24) and says to mix all of them:

| Type | Purpose | Wick's current share |
|---|---|---|
| **Inspirational** | Moves people, builds emotional connection | ~100% |
| **Conversational** | Invites response, builds community | ~0% |
| **Promotional** | Drives a specific action | 0% |

"A feed that's only promotional stops getting engagement" — the inverse is also
true. A feed that never invites response does not build a community, it builds
an audience of strangers.

**Important honesty check:** the captions DO end with an engagement question,
so this is not zero by design. But at 30 reach, 1 comment is arithmetically
unremarkable — roughly 0.04% of total lifetime reach, against a typical
0.1–0.5%. **Low, but the dominant cause is that almost nobody has seen it.**
Do not "fix" the questions before fixing reach; that would be treating a
symptom that is mostly just small numbers.

## THE RULE OF SEVEN — why nothing has landed

> "A potential customer must see a message at least seven times before they're
> ready to take action." — Section 23

At a median 27 reach with almost no repeat exposure, **essentially nobody
outside the 41 followers has encountered this brand seven times.** That single
line explains six weeks of flat results better than any critique of the hooks.

It also sets the near-term strategy: **frequency and consistency beat one-off
brilliance.** Which matches the independent research finding that daily posting
grows 3–5× faster than 2–3×/week.

## THE GOALS CASCADE — answered 2026-09-12

The course requires *business goal → marketing goals → social goals → metrics*,
and every metric traceable up that chain. Dre's answers complete it:

**BUSINESS GOAL** — build an audience for a digital product (product TBD; the
list is how he'll decide what to build).

**MARKETING GOAL** — grow reach enough that the rule of seven can operate at
all. Nothing else is reachable from 27 median reach.

**SOCIAL GOAL (SMART)** — *Raise median reach per post from 27 to 90+ within
three weeks of daily posting, on US audiences, by flipping the format mix to
60–70% Reels.*
- Specific: median reach per post
- Measurable: 27 → 90 (the insights API already reports it)
- Attainable: Reels average 3–5× carousel reach; his own 6 videos averaged 120
  against 28 for carousels, so the multiplier is already demonstrated on THIS
  account
- Relevant: reach is upstream of every other number, including list signups
- Time-bound: three weeks

**KPI** — median reach per post. Not average: one 1,444 outlier distorts the
mean badly, and this account has already proved that.

**SECONDARY METRICS** — saves (intent to return) and shares (the growth engine,
24 across 46 posts). Both watched, neither judged yet; they move after reach.
